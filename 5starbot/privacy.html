5★ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Overview

5★ (“the Bot”) is a private helper and utility bot operated for the 5$TAR / PlaqueBoyMax Discord community. It is not offered as a public multi-server product.

Utilizing the Bot and its features automatically acknowledges this policy.

Who this applies to

Members and staff of Discord servers where 5★ is a member of for staff and community utility features. This policy does not apply to users who are not members of the server or staff.

Data We Collect

Discord account / member data

  • User IDs, usernames, and nicknames
  • Role IDs and role membership needed for permissions and staff tools
  • Server, channel, and configuration IDs used to run the Bot

Message content (limited cases only)

  • Commands you run with the Bot (including arguments)
  • Messages in strike appeal threads you open (including attachments), so they can be reviewed
  • Limited message content in other safety contexts where needed to protect the server

Community features

  • Reaction leaderboard scores and related settings

We do not read your DMs, monitor every message in the server, or use your data to train ML/AI models.

Use, Storage & Sharing

How we use the data

  • Track and review staff records and appeals
  • Run utility tools such as 'SOS/Alert'
  • Check user and role permissions for authorized access
  • Keep community and staff features working
  • Help keep the server safe and the Bot running reliably

Where data is stored

Bot data is stored off Discord locally on the Bot's system. This can include staff records, transcripts, and operational logs.

Access is strictly limited as needed for staff management and maintenance.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with authorized server staff for staff management, if required by law or to protect safety, or when it is already visible in Discord.

Retention & Your Choices

Retention

  • Records and transcripts are retained so they can be reviewed and referenced
  • Operational logs are limited and may be rotated
  • Data may be removed when it is no longer needed

Exact retention can vary based on the needs of the server and backups.

Your choices

  • Appeals: Opening appeals means your thread messages will be logged.
  • Commands: Avoid putting sensitive personal information in command reasons or arguments.
  • Opt-out: Leaving the server stops future interaction with the Bot there; historical records may still be kept when needed.

To ask about review or deletion of Bot-stored data where feasible, contact server managers. Some records may need to be kept for accountability or safety.

Other

Children’s privacy

The Bot is intended for use in Discord communities that follow Discord’s Terms of Service and age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from children in violation of those rules.

Changes

This policy is subject to change at any time without prior notice. The 'Last updated' date will be updated when changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions about 5★, contact server managers.
